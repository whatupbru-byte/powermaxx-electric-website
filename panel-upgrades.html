When a panel or service upgrade may be needed

An electrical panel distributes power throughout the property. A qualified assessment can determine whether the existing equipment and service capacity match the planned electrical load.

  • Renovations, additions, basement finishing, or new major appliances
  • EV charger, hot tub, pool equipment, electric heating, or workshop loads
  • Older, damaged, crowded, or difficult-to-service panel equipment
  • Repeated electrical concerns that require proper troubleshooting
  • Planning for future electrical demand

What the process includes

PowerMaxx begins by reviewing the existing service, panel, circuits, planned loads, available space, and installation conditions. The recommended scope depends on the property and the work being added.

Careful planning

The job is planned around safe access, practical routing, equipment requirements, and coordination needed for the project.

Installation and testing

New equipment is installed, identified, connected, and tested as part of the completed electrical work.

Frequently asked questions

Does every EV charger require a panel upgrade?

No. The answer depends on the existing service, current loads, charger requirements, and the installation design. An assessment is needed before deciding.

Can a panel be upgraded during a renovation?

Yes. Renovations are a common time to review capacity and coordinate new circuits or equipment with the larger project.
