Signs you may need a panel upgrade

  • Breakers trip often or will not reset
  • Lights dim or flicker when appliances start
  • Your panel is warm, buzzing, discoloured, or showing signs of damage
  • You still have an older fuse panel
  • You are adding an EV charger, hot tub, air conditioner, suite, or major new appliance
  • You are planning a kitchen, basement, or whole-home renovation

If you smell burning, see smoke, or notice sparking around electrical equipment, treat it as an urgent safety issue and call an emergency electrician right away.

Panel upgrade FAQs

Do I need a 200-amp service?

It depends on your present load and future plans. A load calculation helps determine whether a service upgrade is needed, particularly when adding high-demand equipment such as an EV charger, hot tub, or major addition.

Can I install an EV charger with my current panel?

Sometimes. We assess available capacity and can recommend a dedicated circuit, load-management option, sub-panel, or service upgrade as appropriate.

How long does a panel upgrade take?

The timing depends on the condition of the existing system, the scope of the upgrade, and any required coordination. We provide a project-specific schedule after the assessment.

Do I need a licensed electrician for panel work?

Panel and service work should be handled by a qualified, licensed electrical contractor because it involves critical safety and code requirements.
