Electrical problems that need prompt attention

  • Burning odour, smoke, heat or visible arcing near electrical equipment
  • Partial or complete loss of power not caused by a utility outage
  • Breakers that trip repeatedly or will not reset
  • Water exposure near panels, receptacles or wiring
  • Damaged service equipment, wiring or electrical panels

What PowerMaxx does

The first priority is making the area safe. PowerMaxx then identifies the source of the fault, explains the practical repair options and completes approved work using appropriate electrical methods and materials.

Before calling

Do not touch damaged equipment or enter an area where water and electricity may be present. Keep people away from the affected area. For fire, smoke or immediate danger, leave the building and call emergency services first.

Frequently asked questions

Should I reset a breaker more than once?

No. A breaker that trips again may be responding to a fault or overload that needs diagnosis.

Can a power outage be inside my property?

Yes. A failed breaker, connection, service component or damaged circuit can cause an outage even when neighbouring properties have power.
