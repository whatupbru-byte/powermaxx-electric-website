Electrical problems need systematic testing

Intermittent faults, tripping breakers, failed circuits, flickering lighting, and equipment problems can have more than one possible cause. A proper troubleshooting process helps avoid guessing.

  • Breakers that trip or circuits that stop working
  • Outlets, switches, fixtures, and exterior circuits
  • Lighting and power-distribution concerns
  • Motors, machinery, pumps, and equipment hookups
  • Preventive maintenance and recurring electrical issues

How troubleshooting is approached

Understand the symptoms

When the problem happens, what changed, which equipment is affected, and whether the issue is constant or intermittent all help narrow the search.

Inspect and test

The affected circuit or equipment is reviewed and tested to identify the source of the problem before a repair is proposed.

Repair and verify

After the agreed repair is completed, the affected system is checked to confirm proper operation.

Frequently asked questions

Why does a breaker keep tripping?

Possible causes include overload, a fault, damaged equipment, wiring concerns, or a breaker issue. The cause should be tested rather than assumed.

Can you troubleshoot commercial or industrial equipment?

PowerMaxx Electric works across residential, commercial, and industrial electrical systems, including motors, machinery hookups, and facility support.

What information should I provide?

Describe what is affected, when the issue began, anything that changed beforehand, and whether there are unusual sounds, smells, heat, or repeated tripping. Do not continue using equipment that appears unsafe.
