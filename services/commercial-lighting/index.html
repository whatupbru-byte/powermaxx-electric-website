Commercial lighting services

Good commercial lighting needs to fit the space, the way it is used, and the electrical system serving it. PowerMaxx can help with new installations, lighting upgrades, repairs and circuit work.

  • LED lighting installations and upgrades
  • Office, retail and warehouse lighting
  • Interior and exterior commercial lighting
  • Parking lot and exterior security lighting
  • Lighting controls, switches and dedicated circuits
  • Lighting troubleshooting and repairs

Lighting for renovations and new commercial spaces

For renovations, fit-outs and new installations, PowerMaxx can coordinate lighting circuits and equipment with the broader electrical scope. The installation is planned around access, layout, equipment requirements and the existing or new electrical service.

Serving Toronto and the GTA

Commercial lighting projects are available across Toronto, Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, North York and Etobicoke.

Can you replace older commercial fixtures with LED lighting?

Yes. The existing fixtures, circuits, controls and installation conditions can be assessed to determine an appropriate upgrade scope.

Do you install exterior commercial lighting?

Yes. Commercial exterior and parking-area lighting can be included in an electrical installation or upgrade project.
