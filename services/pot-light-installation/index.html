Lighting designed around the room

Good recessed lighting depends on more than fixture count. Ceiling height, room dimensions, cabinet layout, work surfaces, existing wiring and natural light all affect the final result.

Common installation areas

  • Kitchens, islands and food-preparation areas
  • Basements, family rooms and home offices
  • Bathrooms, hallways and stair areas
  • Retail, office and reception spaces
  • Exterior soffits and covered outdoor areas

Options PowerMaxx can help plan

Projects may include dimmers, multiple lighting zones, low-profile LED fixtures, selectable colour temperature, wet-location fixtures and replacement of outdated lighting.

Frequently asked questions

How many pot lights does a room need?

The answer depends on room size, ceiling height, fixture output and how the space is used. A layout should be planned before holes are cut.

Can pot lights be added without opening the entire ceiling?

Often yes, depending on joist direction, access and the existing wiring route. The site conditions determine the least disruptive approach.
