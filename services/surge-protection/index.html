Professional whole-home surge protection

Modern homes and businesses contain sensitive electronics including appliance controls, HVAC boards, computers, televisions and EV charging equipment. PowerMaxx can assess the electrical panel and select a compatible surge protective device for the installation.

What installation includes

  • Review of the electrical panel and available connection space
  • Selection of a compatible surge protective device
  • Professional installation at the service equipment
  • Verification of the installation and device status
  • Recommendations for additional point-of-use protection where useful

A layered approach to electrical protection

Whole-home protection does not replace every plug-in protector, but it can reduce the energy reaching branch circuits. Important electronics may benefit from both panel-level and point-of-use protection.

Frequently asked questions

Does surge protection prevent all electrical damage?

No device can guarantee protection from every event, but a properly selected and installed system can improve protection against common transient surges.

Can whole-home surge protection be installed on an older panel?

Sometimes. Panel condition, compatibility and available space should be checked first. An upgrade may be recommended if the equipment is obsolete or unsafe.
