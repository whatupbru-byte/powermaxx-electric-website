Electrical work for Toronto properties

Toronto includes older houses, renovated semis, condominiums, retail spaces and industrial buildings, each with different electrical demands. PowerMaxx reviews the existing system, identifies practical options and completes approved work with attention to safety, access and future use.

Common Toronto electrical services

  • Electrical repairs, outage diagnosis and breaker problems
  • Panel upgrades for renovations, additions and EV charging
  • Pot lights, fixtures, dimmers and exterior lighting
  • Kitchen, basement and whole-home renovation wiring
  • Commercial lighting, power distribution and equipment connections
  • Whole-home surge protection and safety upgrades

Planning work in an older Toronto home

Older properties may have limited panel capacity, mixed wiring methods or previous alterations. Before adding major loads, PowerMaxx can assess the available service, circuit condition and routing options so the project is planned around the actual building.
