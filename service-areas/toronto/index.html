Toronto electrician FAQs

What types of electrical work do you handle in Toronto?

We handle common residential and commercial electrical needs, including panel upgrades, EV charger installations, troubleshooting, repairs, pot lights, surge protection, and urgent electrical service.

Can you help with electrical work during a renovation?

Yes. We can assess existing capacity, plan new circuits, update panels where needed, and complete electrical work for kitchens, basements, additions, and other renovation projects.

When should I call an emergency electrician?

Call promptly if you notice smoke, burning odours, sparking, damaged wiring, a hot panel, or breakers that keep tripping. If there is an immediate danger, keep clear of the affected area and contact emergency services as appropriate.
