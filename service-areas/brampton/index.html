Frequently asked questions

Can you wire a finished basement?

Yes. PowerMaxx can plan receptacles, lighting, smoke alarms and dedicated circuits around the basement layout and intended use.

How do I know whether I need a panel upgrade?

Warning signs can include limited breaker space, planned high-demand equipment or an older service that does not match the property's needs. A site assessment is the best way to confirm.
