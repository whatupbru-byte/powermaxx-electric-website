Frequently asked questions

Do I need to buy the charger first?

Not necessarily. It is helpful to know the vehicle and preferred charger, but reviewing the property first can prevent choosing equipment that does not suit the available installation.

Will I need a panel upgrade?

Some properties do and some do not. The existing service, other electrical loads, and charger requirements must be reviewed.

Can the charger be installed outside?

Outdoor installation may be possible when the equipment and installation method are suitable for the location. The site conditions need to be assessed.
