Our EV charger installation process

  1. Consultation: Tell us about your vehicle, charger, parking arrangement, and charging goals.
  2. Site and load assessment: We assess your panel, available capacity, route for wiring, and charger location.
  3. Clear scope and quote: We explain the recommended work before installation begins.
  4. Installation and testing: A licensed electrician installs the circuit and charger connection, then tests the system.
  5. Walk-through: We review the completed work and answer basic use questions.

Why use a licensed electrician?

  • Safe electrical capacity:
    EV charging can add substantial continuous demand to a home's electrical system.
  • Proper circuit sizing:
    Equipment, wiring, breakers, and protection must match the charger and site conditions.
  • Code-conscious installation:
    Professional installation helps ensure the work is completed with applicable requirements in mind.
  • Future-ready planning:
    We can assess whether your current panel supports future electrical needs as well as EV charging.

EV charger FAQs

Do I need a panel upgrade for an EV charger?

Not always. A load assessment determines whether your existing electrical service has sufficient available capacity. If it does not, we can recommend a panel upgrade, load-management option, or another appropriate solution.

Can I install an EV charger in a condo?

Many condo installations are possible, but they require coordination with the condominium corporation or property manager and may involve site-specific electrical planning.

What charger should I buy?

The right charger depends on your vehicle, desired charging speed, available electrical capacity, parking location, and features you want. We can discuss the installation requirements before you purchase.

How do I get a quote?

Call 416-697-4671 or use our contact form. Include your location, vehicle or charger details, parking setup, and photos of your panel if available.
