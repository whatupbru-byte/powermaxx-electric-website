When to call an electrician urgently

  • Burning smells, smoke, or visible sparking
  • A hot, buzzing, or damaged electrical panel
  • Outlets or switches that feel warm, scorch, or show discolouration
  • Repeated breaker trips that return immediately after resetting
  • Water affecting electrical equipment or wiring
  • Sudden partial power loss with no obvious external cause

If there is an immediate risk of fire or injury, move to a safe area and contact emergency services. For urgent electrical issues, contact our emergency electrician service.

Electrical repair FAQs

Why does my breaker keep tripping?

A breaker may trip because of an overloaded circuit, a faulty appliance, a wiring problem, or a device issue. Repeated trips should be assessed rather than ignored.

Can I reset a tripped breaker?

You can reset a breaker once if it is safe to do so, but do not repeatedly reset a breaker that trips again. That can indicate an issue requiring professional inspection.

Why do my lights flicker?

Flickering can be caused by a loose bulb or fixture, a circuit problem, an overloaded connection, or an issue with the electrical service. A proper assessment identifies the cause.
